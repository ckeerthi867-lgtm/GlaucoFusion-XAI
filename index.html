GlaucoFusion-XAI Project Information

Abstract Overview

Glaucoma is an ophthalmic condition characterized by progressive optic nerve damage, often correlated with elevated intraocular pressure. Conventional screening relies heavily on manual, subjective fundus photograph interpretation. GlaucoFusion-XAI introduces an explainable multimodal framework combining deep CNNs (VGG16 transfer learning) and Vision Transformers with LIME/Grad-CAM interpretability and generative LLM clinical summaries.

Current Project Scope: Stage 1 Prototype

This interactive interface demonstrates the complete clinical workflow: registration, authentication, patient data ingestion, retinal upload and inspection, AI processing simulation, XAI heatmap exploration, and clinical interpretation reporting with zero backend or fake medical values.

Authors & Academic Context: Final-Year Project • Computer Science & Engineering
